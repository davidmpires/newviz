The policy, as written

For employees subject to this policy, individual badge-in data (including use of a loaner badge) will be used to measure attendance in offices where employees are able to swipe their EG issued badge for entry.

Your office presence is calculated using badge-in data and a methodology called “Best Eight, Last Twelve”:

  • Your badge-in data is collected over a rolling 12-week period and we eliminate the 4 weeks with your lowest attendance. This accounts for paid time off, holidays, sick time, travel to EG offices and offsite business events where badge data isn’t available.
  • We count the number of days you badged into the office over the remaining 8 weeks (with a maximum 5 badge-ins counted per week).
  • If you work a 5-day work week and you badged into an EG office 24 days or more over those “best” 8 weeks (3+ days on average per week), you are in compliance with the policy.

The sum. Rank the last 12 weeks by badge-ins, drop the 4 lowest, add up the rest. That total has to reach 24. No week counts for more than 5.

Time away is not a separate category. The 4 dropped weeks are how the policy absorbs leave, sickness, travel and offsites. A week away is simply a low week. Five weeks away in one window means one of them is counted, and the other weeks have to make up for it. Time away and bank holidays here only limit the days you can go in when planning; they never change the target.

A planning aid, not the system of record. Check anything close against your own badge data.
