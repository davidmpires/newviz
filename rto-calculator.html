Data, policy and how it works

Your office presence is calculated using badge-in data and a methodology called “Best Eight, Last Twelve”:

  • Your badge-in data is collected over a rolling 12-week period and we eliminate the 4 weeks with your lowest attendance. This accounts for paid time off, holidays, sick time, travel to EG offices and offsite business events where badge data isn’t available.
  • We count the number of days you badged into the office over the remaining 8 weeks (with a maximum 5 badge-ins counted per week).
  • If you work a 5-day work week and you badged into an EG office 24 days or more over those “best” 8 weeks (3+ days on average per week), you are in compliance with the policy.

The sum. Rank the last 12 weeks by badge-ins, drop the 4 lowest, add up the rest; it has to reach 24. Time away is not deducted anywhere. A week away is just a low week, and the 4 dropped weeks absorb it. Five weeks away in one window means one of them counts as a zero. Time away and bank holidays here only block days in the plan. Each Monday the window moves on a week.
A planning aid, not the system of record.
